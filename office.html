How to Explore (Visitor Controls):
• Click Any Character or Desk: Opens the portfolio summary card on the right with direct links to open the Scholar Preprint or view GitHub code.

• Show / Hide Panels: Use ☰ Left, 📋 Right, and 🎛️ Bottom in the top bar (or the ✕ buttons) to show or hide the left panel, right panel, bottom camera dock, and live notification bar anytime.
